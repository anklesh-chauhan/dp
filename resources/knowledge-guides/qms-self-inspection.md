# Self-Inspection / Internal Audit

Use **Internal Audits** and **Audit Findings** under **QMS** for self-inspection programmes: schedule, conduct, report, follow up, and close audits with finding disposition and optional CAPA handoff.

The customer remains responsible for validated intended use and for physical GMP Premises / Plant compliance.

## Where to work

| Screen | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| **Internal Audits** | Plan scope, criteria, schedule, execution, reporting, and closure. |
| **Audit Findings** | Record nonconformities, responses, verification, and disposition. |
| **CAPAs** | Open when an Audit Finding→CAPA handoff creates a corrective/preventive action. |

## Typical path

1. Create an internal audit with scope, objectives, criteria, and lead auditor.
2. Schedule and conduct the audit; attach plans, notes, and objective evidence.
3. Raise findings with severity and classification; request and verify responses.
4. Hand off actionable nonconformities to CAPA when required.
5. Close the audit only when every finding is closed, rejected, or cancelled.

## Related QMS topics

- **Complaint Handling** — market feedback that may trigger audits or CAPA.
- **Schedule M Gap Assessment** — self-inspection as a Part I PQS topic.
- **Management Review** — audit outcomes as a quality-system input.
