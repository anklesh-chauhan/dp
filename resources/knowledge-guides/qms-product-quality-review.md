# Product Quality Review (PQR)

Use **Product Quality Reviews** under **QMS** for attributable annual (or periodic) product reviews with input summaries, signed approval, and closure.

The customer remains responsible for validated intended use and for physical GMP Premises / Plant compliance.

## Where to work

| Screen | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Quality Reviews** | Create the period package, advance review, approve, close, and attach evidence. |
| **Quality Metrics** | View PQR lifecycle counts and overdue in-progress reviews (permission gated). |
| **CAPAs** / **Change Controls** | Open when spawn stubs create follow-up quality work from the review. |

## Typical path

1. Create a PQR for a product and review period with clear ownership.
2. Capture period inputs and narrative as your procedures require.
3. Move through under-review and signed approval transitions with attributable reasons.
4. Close the review when outputs and signatures meet your gate criteria.
5. Raise CAPA or change control follow-up from the review when your PQS requires it.

## Related QMS topics

- **Management Review** — PQR outputs as a required quality-system input.
- **Schedule M Gap Assessment** — Product Quality Review as a Part I clause topic.
- **Change Control** and **CAPA** — follow-up from annual review conclusions.
