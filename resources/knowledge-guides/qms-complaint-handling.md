# Complaint Handling

Use **Complaints** under **QMS** to intake, assess, investigate, respond to, and close product or process complaints with attributable reasons and immutable history.

The customer remains responsible for validated intended use and for physical GMP Premises / Plant compliance.

## Where to work

| Screen | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| **Complaints** | Create intake records, advance the lifecycle, attach evidence, and view signed history. |
| **Deviations** | Open when a Complaint→Deviation handoff creates a linked quality event. |
| **Product Recalls** | Open when a safety or regulatory path requires a market recall handoff. |

## Typical path

1. Create a complaint with source, type, product/batch context, and ownership.
2. Assess safety and regulatory implications before investigation or response.
3. Investigate and respond through permission-gated lifecycle actions only.
4. Close only when required assessment and response evidence gates are met.
5. Use handoffs when a Deviation or Product Recall is required — do not duplicate facts outside the linked record.
