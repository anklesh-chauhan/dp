# Product Recall & Returns

Use **Product Recalls** and **Product Returns** under **QMS** for market recall and return disposition. These records are distinct from **DMS** controlled-copy issuance recall.

The customer remains responsible for validated intended use and for physical GMP Premises / Plant compliance.

## Where to work

| Screen | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Recalls** | Classify and advance market recalls, including mock-recall exercises and complaint linkage. |
| **Product Returns** | Record returned product, QA disposition, and signed lifecycle history. |
| **Complaints** | Source handoff when a complaint requires a market recall. |

## Typical path

1. Create a recall or return with product, batch, and ownership context.
2. Advance through the permitted lifecycle with attributable reasons on every transition.
3. Attach evidence packages privately; preserve integrity hashes.
4. Complete QA disposition for returns; close recalls only through signed gates.
5. Do not use DMS issuance recall for market actions — keep document-copy recall separate.

## Related QMS topics

- **Complaint Handling** — intake that may trigger recall.
- **Deviations** / **CAPA** — quality events that accompany market actions when required.
- **Schedule M Gap Assessment** — recalls and returns as Part I PQS topics.
