# QualiGxP must not claim regulatory certification

QualiGxP is **designed to support** customer compliance with 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, cGMP, and ICH Q10. It is **not certified**, **not validated out of the box**, and **not itself cGMP- or ICH Q10-compliant**.

Use “supports”, “aligned with”, or “designed to support”. Never write “compliant”, “certified”, “Part 11 validated”, “Annex 11 certified”, or “cGMP certified” about the product.

The customer remains responsible for validated intended use, site procedures, and inspection claims. Public copy lives in `website/compliance.html` and must stay consistent with that position.
