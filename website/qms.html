Optional module · requires DMS

A pharmaceutical quality system your plant can actually run.

QualiGxP QMS is built for Indian SME quality teams: planned change and unplanned events in the same workspace as document control. Every consequential transition needs an attributable reason and is written to immutable history. Use it for domestic Schedule M work and for WHO / US / EU-oriented sites. The customer remains responsible for validated intended use and for physical GMP Premises and Plant compliance.

Weekly quality work

Change, deviation, investigation, and CAPA — connected.

Change control

Draft through approval, implementation, effectiveness review, and closure. Link affected controlled documents and create traced DMS revisions for approved “revise” impacts. Major and critical changes can require linked residual-risk assessment before approval.

Deviations

Record contemporaneous facts for unplanned departures. Move from open through investigation, CAPA, effectiveness review, and closure. Critical deviations can require a linked risk assessment before investigation completes.

Investigations

Structured root-cause work linked to the parent deviation. The deviation cannot complete investigation while required linked investigations remain open.

CAPA

Plan, implement, and verify effectiveness. If the check fails, mark the CAPA ineffective, revise the plan, and implement again before closure.

Full QMS workspace

Every quality process that used to sit in a spreadsheet.

Complaints, self-inspection, risk, suppliers, and management review are live QMS workspaces — the same entitlement, permissions, signed transitions, and private evidence as deviations.

Complaints

Intake and assess market complaints with product and batch traceability. Hand off an assessed product-quality complaint to a Deviation, or a recall-worthy complaint to Product Recall.

Self-inspection

Plan and close internal audits and findings. Actionable nonconformities can spawn a CAPA. An audit cannot close while findings remain open.

Quality risk management

Score initial and residual risk, then link assessments to change, deviation, CAPA, complaint, audit finding, investigation, and CSV projects.

Supplier qualification

Qualify, periodically review, suspend, or disqualify vendors with signed decisions and audit evidence for high and critical risk.

Management review

Run reviews with required PQS inputs, decisions, actions, and issued minutes. Assemble suggested inputs from live quality metrics and download a review pack.

Quality metrics

Lifecycle counts and overdue workload across quality events — integers only, no narrative leakage — plus a Schedule M readiness view for gap, calibrations, and critical findings.

Laboratory OOS / OOT

Phase I / Phase II laboratory events with investigation and Deviation gates, private evidence, and signed history before confirmed closure.

04

Equipment, VMP, and qualification

Validation Master Plans, equipment assets, and DQ / IQ / OQ / PQ plus process and cleaning qualifications — distinct from computerised-system CSV.

05

Calibration and preventive maintenance

Calibration schedules and PM work orders on assets. Out-of-tolerance requires a linked Deviation. Overdue calibration can block critical execution approval.

06

Training and competency

Role curricula linked to effective SOPs, requalification, and competency checks on execution and change-control approval when the gate is enabled.

07

Gap assessment, SMF, inspector pack

Schedule M Part I clause checklist, signed Site Master File, and an entitlement-gated inspector evidence pack for CDSCO inspection prep.

08

Computer system validation

Risk-based CSV projects with approved requirements, reviewed executions, accepted residual risk, closed deviations, and independent QA release.
