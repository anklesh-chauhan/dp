Quality starts with control

Built for attributable, durable records.

QualiGxP is designed around electronic records, electronic signatures, and data-integrity practices expected in pharmaceutical operations. It supports work aligned with 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, ALCOA+, and WHO GMP — with the customer remaining responsible for validated intended use.

Electronic signatures

Approvals, rejections, returns, and other consequential decisions capture signer identity, meaning, timestamp, reason, client metadata, and a verifiable signature hash.

Append-only history

Lifecycle events are written as immutable audit history. Approved content is revised, not overwritten. Failed validation runs are preserved.

Separation of duties

Permissions, roles, and department scope control who can act. Authors cannot approve their own submission. Independent verification is required where GMP work demands it.

Private files

Controlled PDFs, original artifacts, and execution evidence are stored privately. Evidence carries metadata and a SHA-256 integrity hash.

Licensed modules

DMS, QMS, and AI are server-enforced entitlements. Production licences are cryptographically verified. Invalid, expired, or incomplete licences are rejected.

Validated use

CSV Validation Projects provide requirement-to-test traceability, independent review, residual-risk acceptance, and a signed QA release for computerized systems.

Data-integrity practices the system supports

The record should say who did what, when, and why.

PracticeHow QualiGxP supports it
AttributableNamed user accounts, roles, and signed decisions. Shared accounts are not used for approval work.
LegibleRendered controlled copies, issuance numbers, watermarks, and printable validation summaries.
ContemporaneousRequired reasons and timestamps on lifecycle transitions as the work happens.
OriginalMasters, issued copies, and execution records are distinct. Evidence belongs to the execution, not an overwritten master.
AccurateIndependent verification, numeric acceptance criteria, material reconciliation, and blocked actions that name the incomplete field.
Complete and enduringRetention, archival, and destruction are controlled states. History remains after a document is superseded or obsolete.

QualiGxP is software. Validation for a specific intended use, local procedures, and regulatory submissions remain the customer’s responsibility.
