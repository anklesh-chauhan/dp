Designed to support regulated work

Built for attributable, durable records.

QualiGxP is designed to support customer compliance with 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, cGMP, and ICH Q10 — alongside ALCOA+, WHO GMP, and revised Schedule M / CDSCO. It is not certified, not validated out of the box, and not itself cGMP- or ICH Q10-compliant. The customer remains responsible for validated intended use, site procedures, inspection claims, and physical GMP Premises / Plant compliance.

Electronic signatures

Approvals, rejections, returns, and other consequential decisions capture signer identity, meaning, timestamp, reason, client metadata, and a verifiable signature hash.

Append-only history

Lifecycle events are written as append-only audit history. Approved content is revised, not overwritten. Failed validation runs are preserved.

Separation of duties

Permissions, roles, and department scope control who can act. Authors cannot approve their own submission. Independent verification is required where GMP work demands it.

Private files

Controlled PDFs, original artifacts, and execution evidence are stored privately. Evidence carries metadata and a SHA-256 integrity hash.

Licensed modules

DMS, QMS, and AI are server-enforced entitlements. Production licences are cryptographically verified. Invalid, expired, or incomplete licences are rejected.

CSV for intended use

CSV Validation Projects provide requirement-to-test traceability, independent review, residual-risk acceptance, and a signed QA release so the customer can validate a computerised system for its intended use.

Frameworks QualiGxP is designed to support

Controls in the product. Claims stay with the site.

QualiGxP is designed to support these frameworks. It is not a substitute for validated intended use, site procedures, or an inspector’s judgement of the plant.

FrameworkHow QualiGxP supports customer work
21 CFR Part 11Named unique accounts, password re-entry on electronic signatures, signature manifestation on controlled copies, append-only audit history, access lockout, and optional MFA. Customers validate intended use of these controls.
EU GMP Annex 11Security and identity controls, electronic signatures, audit trails, change records, computerised-system incidents, independent batch-release records, and CSV projects. Backup, clock sync, TLS, and disaster recovery remain site procedures.
cGMPDocument control, issuance, BMR/BPR execution, deviations, CAPA, complaints, laboratory OOS, and related quality records. QualiGxP does not make a plant cGMP-compliant. Premises, utilities, and manufacturing practice stay with the site.
ICH Q10Pharmaceutical quality-system processes: change management, CAPA, management review, product quality review, quality risk management, and lessons learned. PQS effectiveness remains an organisational claim.

QualiGxP is software. It is not certified against 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, cGMP, or ICH Q10. Validation for a specific intended use, local procedures, and any statement to an inspector remain the customer’s responsibility.

Data-integrity practices the system supports

The record should say who did what, when, and why.

PracticeHow QualiGxP supports it
AttributableNamed user accounts, roles, and signed decisions. Shared accounts are not used for approval work.
LegibleRendered controlled copies, issuance numbers, watermarks, and printable validation summaries.
ContemporaneousRequired reasons and timestamps on lifecycle transitions as the work happens.
OriginalMasters, issued copies, and execution records are distinct. Evidence belongs to the execution, not an overwritten master.
AccurateIndependent verification, numeric acceptance criteria, material reconciliation, and blocked actions that name the incomplete field.
Complete and enduringRetention, archival, and destruction are controlled states. History remains after a document is superseded or obsolete.

ALCOA+ is a data-integrity practice the records support. It is not a product certification.
