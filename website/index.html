Who it is for

The teams that actually run an Indian plant.

Document control

SOP authors, checkers, and document controllers managing templates, masters, issuance, and retention.

Quality assurance

QA heads and officers handling deviations, CAPA, change control, self-inspection, and PQR without a large corporate QMS staff.

Production GMP

Shop-floor teams completing issued BMR, BPR, log, form, and checklist records with independent verification.

QC and validation

Laboratory OOS/OOT, equipment qualification, calibration, and computerised-system validation with signed gates.

QualiGxP records pharmaceutical quality-system work. It does not replace site responsibility for Premises, Plant, utilities, or physical equipment compliance.
