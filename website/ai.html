Optional module · requires DMS

AI that assists. People who decide.

When the AI module is licensed, QualiGxP can help a small QA team draft regulated templates faster. Generated content is assistance, not an approval decision and not a replacement for qualified review. Skip this module until the document-control process is already in place.

Template generation

Template creation can start regulatory template generation in the background. If AI is not licensed, the template is saved normally and completed by the author.

Draft assistance

Controlled-document drafting assistance helps quality teams structure SOP and record content faster, then returns the work to the same review and approval workflow.

Observable execution

AI execution status and provider performance are visible under AI Management, so assistance remains reviewable rather than hidden.

Responsible by design

Assistance inside a controlled system.

AI sits on top of DMS. Templates and documents still require the same permissions, separation of duties, attributable reasons, and append-only history as work created by hand. For most SME plants, DMS and QMS come first.

Human in the loop

Authors, checkers, and approvers remain the decision-makers. AI output must be reviewed before it can become an approved controlled record.

Add AI only when your quality process is ready for it.
