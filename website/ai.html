Optional module · requires DMS

AI that assists. People who decide.

When the AI module is licensed, QualiGxP adds a read-only application assistant for everyday plant questions, optional drafting help for controlled documents, and background template generation. Every answer and draft is assistance — not an approval decision and not a replacement for qualified review. Skip this module until document control is already in place.

QualiGxP Assistant

A read-only chat assistant searches approved and effective controlled documents, permitted QMS records, and your pending approval queue — then links every record-specific answer to its source.

Document draft assistant

Controlled-document drafting runs as queued background work with visible status. Authors review the preview, then create a draft that enters the same review and approval workflow as any other document.

Template generation

Template creation can start regulatory template generation in the background. If AI is not licensed, the template is saved normally and completed by the author.

Observable execution

AI execution status, provider routing, and rate limits are visible under AI Management, so assistance remains reviewable rather than hidden.

Responsible by design

Assistance inside a controlled system.

AI sits on top of DMS and, when licensed, QMS. Provider routing respects data-classification rules, requests are rate-limited, and every execution is recorded. Templates and documents still require the same permissions, separation of duties, attributable reasons, and append-only history as work created by hand. For most SME plants, DMS and QMS come first.

Human in the loop

Authors, checkers, and approvers remain the decision-makers. AI output must be reviewed before it can become an approved controlled record. The application assistant summarizes authorized records only — it never replaces qualified personnel.
