Optional module · requires DMS

AI that assists. People who decide.

When the AI module is licensed, QualiGxP can help generate regulated templates and monitor AI execution. Generated content is assistance, not an approval decision and not a replacement for qualified review.

Template generation

Template creation can start regulatory template generation in the background. If AI is not licensed, the template is saved normally and completed by the author.

Draft assistance

Controlled-document drafting assistance helps quality teams structure content faster, then returns the work to the same review and approval workflow.

Observable execution

AI execution status and provider performance are visible under AI Management, so assistance remains reviewable rather than hidden.

What AI does not do

01
It does not approve

Electronic signatures stay with authorized people

02
It does not skip workflow

Drafts still move through configured steps

03
It does not replace review

Qualified personnel remain accountable

Responsible by design

Assistance inside a controlled system.

AI sits on top of DMS. Templates and documents still require the same permissions, separation of duties, attributable reasons, and append-only history as work created by hand.

Human in the loop

Authors, checkers, and approvers remain the decision-makers. AI output must be reviewed before it can become an approved controlled record.

Add AI only when your quality process is ready for it.
