Required core module

Document control built for GMP work.

QualiGxP DMS manages templates, controlled-document masters, approvals, training, issuance, GMP execution records, revision, archival, and retention. It is the required core of the platform.

Lifecycle

From draft to destruction, with a reason at every gate.

Draft → Under review → Approved → Required training → Effective → Issued copy → Revision or obsolete → Archived → Retention completed → Destroyed.

Templates

Author reusable SOP and record templates, submit them through department or global workflows, and publish only after every mandatory step is approved.

Controlled masters

Create documents from published templates. Editing locks on submission. Reviewers approve, return, or reject with attributable electronic signatures.

Effectiveness

Approved is not the same as effective. Required training and Document Control confirmation of the effective date happen before operational use.

Issuance

Issue a read-only reference copy or a writable GMP execution copy. Each copy carries an issuance number and watermark. Recalled copies cannot be printed.

Execution records

Forms, logs, checklists, BMRs, and BPRs capture data on the issued copy, not the master. Independent verification and QA disposition are enforced where required.

Retention

Supersede, obsolete, archive, complete retention, and destroy through controlled actions so history remains intact.

Controls that stay with the record

Separation of duties is not optional.

The author or submitter cannot decide an approval step. Each step in a cycle must be decided by a different user. For BMR/BPR, the executor, production reviewer, and QA approver must be different people.

Also included

Department and global approval workflows, number series, regulation tags, controlled PDF rendering, document import from PDF/DOC/DOCX, and append-only audit history for every consequential action.
